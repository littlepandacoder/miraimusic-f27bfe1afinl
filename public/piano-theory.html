Create the Scale

Click any note head to cycle ♮ → ♯ → ♭ and build the correct major scale.
Hit Check Scale to see which notes are right.

Major scale pattern: W W H W W W H W = whole step (2 semitones) · H = half step (1 semitone)
Tips: Every major scale follows W W H W W W H. Half steps always fall between degrees 3→4 and 7→8. Work out which notes need accidentals so those half steps land in the right place.
